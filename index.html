What is Lunoia?

Lunoia is a lightweight, privacy-first AI that runs entirely on your local machine. No cloud dependency. No telemetry. Just clean, efficient code. Built for those who believe intelligence should be accessible, private, and free.

Core Features

  • Ultra-Lightweight i think: Designed to run smoothly on <512MB RAM environments.
  • 100% Local & Private: Your data never leaves your device.
